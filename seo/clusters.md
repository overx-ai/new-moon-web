# Topic clusters

Restructured 2026-09-07 against measured ASO data and web SERPs. See `keywords.md` for the
numbers and their provenance.

**The ordering principle changed.** This is no longer "an English pillar, translated four
times". Demand is five times higher in German than English, and the German SERP wants
different content from the English one, so each locale gets its own target and its own
angle. Only `/fr` is a translation, because it has no data.

---

## B. What the day is good for (supporting)

The cluster that carries the actual differentiator: 30 lunar days, 8 activities, 240 rated
pairs. Nobody in any storefront's top 25 does this.

- **`/lunar-days`** — all 30 in one place, targeting `30 lunar days` and `moon guide`
  (18/19). The SERP has a dozen sites with one thin page per day and no single
  comprehensive one. **Written 2026-09-07.** Links out to the pillar `/` and to `/de`
  (cluster A). Linked in from the English homepage features section; the other four home
  pages do not link it, because the page is English only.
- Candidates, unwritten: `lucky days` (25/23, the best US ratio), `biorhythm` (16/13),
  `energy tracker` (15/23), and German `günstige tage` (diff 12, three competing apps).

## C. Widgets (supporting, unwritten)

`moon widget` (22/48 us), `widget luna` (5/23 es). Thin as a page on its own. Likely
better as the existing homepage section unless a SERP check says otherwise.

## D. The witchy adjacency — deliberately not a cluster

`crystals` 18/37, `spells` 19/45, `grimoire` 12/19, and Spanish `horoscopo` 43/37 are all
cheap traffic. **We are not writing them.** The app carries a disclaimer that its guidance
is not medical, psychological or financial advice; ranking for spellcraft and horoscopes
invites exactly the audience that disclaimer is there to manage, and it would collide with
the App Store 1.4.1 scrutiny the category already attracts. Recorded here so nobody
rediscovers the numbers and assumes they were missed.

## E. Moon gardening and haircuts — not a cluster, but note the gap

`aussaatkalender` (de), `календарь стрижек` and `посевной календарь` (ru),
`haare schneiden mondkalender` (de, three competing apps). These are real, established
moon habits in the German and Russian markets, at almost zero difficulty.

**The app does not serve them.** It has eight activity types and none of them is planting
or haircuts. Writing pages for them would be a promise the product breaks on open. Left
here as a product observation, not a content plan.

## Not clusters at all

Astronomy and ephemeris precision, eclipse timing, moonrise and moonset tables, natal
charts, and the Chinese lunisolar calendar. The first group is the competitors' ground and
the app forecasts nothing. The last is a different calendar system entirely.
